## Round 1

### claude

## Initial Position on CAN SLIM

Drawing on what's validated in this repo: CAN SLIM here is effectively a **momentum / strength-continuation** strategy, not a value screen. That framing should drive every design decision.

**Core risks I'd flag:**

1. **Zero-signal / overfit trap.** The prior version produced 0 signals in 4 years via over-strict AND-gating of all seven criteria. Requiring every CAN SLIM letter to pass simultaneously is degenerate — real O'Neil practice tolerates partial matches. The fix is the planned **S/A/B/C graded scoring** (match-degree weighting) rather than a hard boolean filter.

2. **Bear-year drawdown is the dominant tail risk** (confirmed over 15yr). This is a *portfolio-level* problem (position sizing, market-regime gate via the "M" — general market direction), **not** something to patch by tightening per-signal thresholds. Don't sacrifice signal precision to dodge a risk that belongs in allocation.

3. **Precision over recall.** The user trades big-swing, low-frequency, and selective — wants *few high-precision* signals. So grade thresholds should be tuned to surface a small S/A tier, not maximize aggregate profit factor or signal count.

**Architectural note:** keep the grading logic as **pure, testable math** (mirroring `tw_indicators.py`), with deterministic fixtures — backtest reproducibility matters more than clever heuristics here. Fundamentals (C/A/N/I) lean on FinMind/FMP, which fall back to mock — be careful that mock data doesn't silently inflate backtest grades.

What specific decision are we resolving — the grading cutoffs, the M-gate, or backtest methodology?

### gemini

From a research perspective, the primary risk with automated CANSLIM screening is **Quantification Bias**—the tendency to over-filter based on rigid metrics while missing the qualitative "DNA" of a true leader.

**Initial Position & Critique:**

1.  **The "Market Direction" (M) Trap:** Most screeners use a binary "Follow-Through Day" (FTD) logic. Research indicates that in high-volatility regimes (like recent years), traditional FTDs yield high false-positive rates. I recommend a **Regime-Switching Model** (e.g., Markov Switching) to confirm "M" rather than simple price/volume thresholds.
2.  **Relative Strength (L) vs. Momentum:** There is a common confusion between O’Neil’s RS Rating (a percentile rank against the entire market) and raw Momentum. A robust "L" implementation must use **Relative Strength Lines** (Price vs. Index) to identify *divergence* before the breakout, rather than just chasing laggards that finally moved.
3.  **Taiwan-Specific "I" (Institutional):** Given the Taiwan market context, the "I" factor cannot rely on quarterly 13F filings. It must track the **"Big Three" (三大法人) net buy/sell** data on a daily/weekly basis. Researching the lead-lag relationship between ITIC (投信) buying and price breakouts is essential for the TWSE/TPEx.
4.  **Alternative: The "Trend Template" Approach:** Instead of hard filters, consider Mark Minervini’s **Trend Template** as a prior-art enhancement. It prioritizes technical alignment (Stage 2) as a prerequisite, which significantly reduces the noise in "C" and "A" fundamental screens.

**Conclusion:** We should move from "Hard Filtering" to **"Multi-Factor Ranking."** This allows us to capture "High-Alpha" candidates that might narrowly miss one rigid O'Neil criterion but exhibit extreme strength in others.

### codex

初步立場：**可以加入 CAN SLIM，但不建議直接承諾完整七因子作為 MVP 功能。**

依目前描述的 FastAPI + Next.js 股票分析架構，`S`（供需/成交量）、`L`（相對強弱）、`M`（市場趨勢）可建立在既有行情與技術指標資料上，實作成本相對低。真正的成本在 `C`、`A`、`I`：季/年 EPS 成長、財報一致化、法人持股或機構參與資料，尤其台股資料來源、授權、公布延遲、股票代碼映射與缺漏處理都會影響可信度。`N` 也不宜假裝可量化，除非另接新聞/事件資料源。

建議分兩階段：

1. `CAN SLIM Lite`：先提供可可靠計算的 `S/L/M`，再加入有明確來源的 `C/A`，每項顯示分數、資料日期與缺資料狀態。
2. 完整版本：確認財報與法人資料供應商後，再加入快取、更新排程、歷史回測與 `I/N`。

若行情資料已存在，Lite 版約 **3-5 個開發日**：後端 scorer/API、測試、前端評分展示。若需新增財報/法人 ingestion，合理估算為 **1-2 週以上**，另有資料授權成本。

核心要求是：CAN SLIM 結果必須可追溯，不能由 AI 直接臆測缺失因子或生成「完整評分」。

### claude

**On the human's steer** — I read it as a reminder of the trading style we've validated: few, high-precision signals, not more candidates. That reframes both critiques.

**gemini:** Agree strongly on the TW "I" factor — daily 三大法人 net buy/sell is the right substrate, and we already have FinMind chip data for it. Agree FTD is noisy. But I **disagree** with the "move to multi-factor ranking to capture high-alpha narrow-misses" conclusion: surfacing candidates that fail a criterion *lowers* precision, which is exactly what the user doesn't want. Ranking ≠ relaxing.

**codex:** Agree the staged Lite MVP (S/L/M first, C/A next, I/N last) fits our mock-fallback architecture and ingestion realities. Sensible sequencing.

**My refined position:** The real tension is zero-signal/overfit vs. precision. The fix isn't ranking-to-include-more — it's the **S/A/B/C grading** we already planned: keep O'Neil criteria strict, but grade by match-degree so 4 years doesn't yield 0 signals. The user still acts only on S/A grades. This preserves precision *and* guarantees a non-degenerate signal stream. Minervini's Trend Template is a good **gate** (Stage-2 prerequisite), not a ranking dimension.

Net: strict criteria + graded output, TW-native I/M, staged build.

### gemini

我同意 Claude 關於「布林過濾（AND-gating）」導致訊號斷流的觀察，S/A/B/C 分級評分是解決過擬合（Overfitting）的最佳路徑。在實作上，我支持 Codex 的 "Lite" 方案，優先落地 S/L/M，因為這三者在台股市場具備高透明度且不依賴延遲的財報。

**補充與修正觀點：**

1.  **N (New Highs) 的量化**：不應依賴新聞 NLP。在 Prior Art（如 Mark Minervini 的 Trend Template）中，N 常被簡化為「股價距離 52 週高點 10% 以內」，這僅需價格資料即可計算。
2.  **I (Institutional) 的代理指標**：若缺乏法人持股明細，研究顯示「量增價漲」的技術特徵（如累積/派發線）是極佳的「機構足跡」代理指標（Proxy），可緩解資料獲取成本。
3.  **M (Market Direction) 的定位**：建議將 M 設為「全局開關」而非加分項。研究重點應放在 M-gate（如大盤處於 50MA 之下）對整體策略的保護效應，而非優化單股評分。

**結論**：應開發「加權評分制」的 Hybrid 模型，並以技術面代理缺失的財報因子，確保回測的連續性。

### codex

我同意 Claude 與 Gemini 的核心方向：CAN SLIM 不應是七項全通過的硬篩選，而應採多因子評分/排名，否則極易再次產生零訊號。台股情境下，`I` 也應優先使用三大法人買賣超，而非套用美股機構資料；`L` 應是相對大盤或同業的強弱排名，不只是股價動能。

我不同意現階段導入 Markov Switching 或直接混入 Minervini Trend Template。前者提高模型、驗證及解釋成本，且在樣本有限時有過擬合風險；後者會讓「CAN SLIM 是否有效」與「技術趨勢過濾是否有效」無法區分。兩者可作後續實驗組，不應成為首版基線。

我的修正立場是：先建立可重現的 CAN SLIM baseline。以 `C/A/N/L/I/S` 產生連續分數與 `S/A/B/C` 等級，`M` 先作簡單、可解釋的市場狀態 gate 或倉位縮放；再針對台股 10 至 15 年資料做 walk-forward backtest，特別檢查空頭年度最大回撤、訊號數、命中率，以及資料缺漏/模擬基本面造成的偏差。首版工作量可控，也能為進階 regime model 提供可信比較基準。
