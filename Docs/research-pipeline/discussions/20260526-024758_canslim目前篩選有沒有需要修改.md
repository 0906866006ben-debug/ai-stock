## Round 1

### claude

Given the garbled topic and my memory of this project, I'll take CAN SLIM screening/grading as the subject.

**My position:**

**1. Don't hard-filter on all seven CAN SLIM criteria.** We already learned (memory: zero-signal guardrail) that strict AND-gating produced 0 signals in 4 years. CAN SLIM's classic form requires *simultaneous* satisfaction of C, A, N, S, L, I, M — that intersection is nearly empty on TW stocks. The S/A/B/C grading direction is the right antidote: score each criterion, weight, sum, then bucket by match-degree. Keep the signal stream non-empty and graded rather than binary.

**2. CAN SLIM is momentum/strength-continuation** (validated, 15yr). So the dominant risk isn't signal precision — it's bear-year drawdown. That belongs at the **portfolio/regime layer** (the "M" = market direction filter), not baked into per-signal gating. Suggest M acts as a position-size/exposure throttle, not a veto.

**3. Data correctness concerns for TW:**
- "A" (annual earnings growth) and "C" (quarterly) need clean FinMind fundamentals — watch for restated/missing quarters producing fake growth spikes. Mock fallback must NOT silently feed the grader fabricated numbers (CLAUDE.md rule). A graded signal built on mock data is worse than no signal.
- "N" (new highs/52-week) and "L" (leader/RS rating) need survivorship-bias-free history and a proper relative-strength universe ranking.

**4. Aligns with user style** (memory): few high-precision signals. So tune grade thresholds for precision on S-grade, let lower grades stay informational.

Open question: which criteria are we actually computing now vs. aspirational?

## Round 2

### claude

Round 2 — my position:

**Agree with codex** on the two highest-priority risks:
1. **PIT correctness first.** If `/tw/screen` and `include_canslim` read *current* fundamentals/revenue while backtesting historical signals, every walk-forward result is look-ahead-biased and the strategy looks better than it is. This must be fixed before any attribution work — it's a correctness bug, not a tuning knob.
2. **Stale-data honesty.** When PIT fundamentals are missing and we fall back to live, the response must flag `stale`/`is_mock` so the AI layer and UI don't present fabricated precision (consistent with our no-invented-facts rule).

**Disagree on sequencing emphasis:** codex frames walk-forward/attribution as the deliverable. Given the user's *few high-precision signals* style and our prior **zero-signal failure**, the priority is the **S/A/B/C graded stream**, not aggregate PF or full attribution. Validate that CANSLIM produces a non-degenerate, non-zero graded signal flow on TW data first; attribution is downstream of that.

**Refinement:** keep CANSLIM flags purely additive on `analyzeTW()` (optional/nullable per schema rules), and gate backtest claims behind PIT data. No new hard AND-gates — grade, don't filter.

## Round 3

### claude

**On the grade inversion (S 0.78 < A 0.99 < B 1.19):** I agree with codex this is the headline finding, and it's damning. If "best match" S underperforms B out-of-sample, then match-degree is *anti-predictive* — the grading axis is mislabeled, not just miscalibrated. We can't paper over it with a regime overlay (risk-on→B, risk-off→S) because that's just refitting the inversion into a story. First prove monotonicity holds OOS before shipping any grade→sizing map.

**Where I push back on my round-2 self:** codex is right that my AND-gating reintroduces the zero-signal failure ([[feedback_zero_signal_overfit]]). I concede — replace hard AND with graded/weighted scoring.

**On `Insufficient_Data`:** agree FinMind gaps must be an explicit grade-suppressing state, never silently treated as "fails criterion." A missing M-quarter ≠ a bad M.

**Refined position:** CANSLIM here is a momentum-continuation strategy ([[project_canslim_score_inversion]]), so its edge is *strength*, not *match-completeness*. The inversion likely means S overfits to peak-extension names that mean-revert. Given the user wants few high-precision signals ([[user_trading_style]]), I'd rather emit fewer B-grade entries with proven OOS PF than many mislabeled S's. Don't ship S/A as live tiers until the ordering survives OOS.

### gemini



### codex

I agree with Claude on both correctness constraints: historical CANSLIM evaluation is invalid until fundamentals and revenue are point-in-time (PIT), and any fallback to current/mock data must be explicitly surfaced as unavailable/stale rather than scored as historical evidence.

I also agree that CANSLIM should be additive and graded, not a new hard gate. A strict AND-filter is likely to reproduce the zero-signal problem, especially in TW coverage where some fields may be sparse.

I only partly disagree on sequencing. A non-degenerate graded signal stream is the first product check, but it is not a substitute for PIT validation: live/diagnostic grading can test coverage and thresholds, while no performance claim or walk-forward comparison should proceed without PIT data.

Refined implementation order:

1. Add nullable CANSLIM component fields plus data-quality/provenance flags to the TW analysis response.
2. Measure field availability and grade distribution on current TW data; tune bands only to avoid trivially zero output.
3. Build PIT retrieval/snapshots for historical fundamentals and monthly revenue.
4. Run walk-forward comparisons of baseline versus additive CANSLIM grading, with attribution by component.

Cost: steps 1-2 are small/medium; PIT storage and historical joins are the dominant engineering effort.
