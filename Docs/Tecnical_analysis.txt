# Technical Analysis Deep Logic Specification

This document defines the detailed technical-analysis logic for the Taiwan Stock AI Decision-Support & Strategy-Validation Platform.

This file is intended for:
- Claude Code
- Codex
- future `TechnicalFeatureBuilder`
- future `TechnicalAnalyzer`
- future `TechnicalAnalysisResult`
- future technical-analysis frontend cards
- future technical-rule backtesting registry

This is a v1 specification.

Every threshold, score weight, rule, and state definition is a v1 hypothesis until historical backtesting validates it.

---

# 0. Core Purpose

Technical analysis in this platform is not a buy/sell signal generator.

Technical analysis answers:

1. What is the current price structure?
2. Is the stock tradable in the short term?
3. Can the current trend continue in the swing / mid-term?
4. Is the long-term technical structure supportive or risky?
5. Is the current move healthy, overheated, weak, or likely to fail?
6. Is price action confirmed by volume?
7. Is the move supported by market / sector / liquidity context?
8. What conditions would invalidate the current technical view?

Technical analysis must always be interpreted together with:

- chip / institutional analysis
- news / catalyst analysis
- fundamental / valuation analysis
- market regime
- sector money flow
- liquidity guard
- day-trading / speculative-noise filter
- calendar distortion filter
- data traceability

Technical analysis alone must never produce a final investment recommendation.

---

# 1. Non-Negotiable Principles

## 1.1 No Single-Indicator Decisions

The system must not output a conclusion based only on:

- MA crossover
- RSI > 70
- RSI < 30
- MACD golden cross
- MACD death cross
- KD golden cross
- KD death cross
- Bollinger upper-band touch
- Bollinger lower-band touch
- one-day volume spike
- one candlestick pattern
- one gap
- one support / resistance touch

These are supporting signals only.

Correct logic:

technical view =
price state
+ trend structure
+ moving average structure
+ volume confirmation
+ support / resistance location
+ breakout / breakdown quality
+ momentum
+ volatility
+ candle behavior
+ market / sector regime
+ liquidity condition
+ invalidation signal

In simple terms:

指標只是證據，不是結論。

---

## 1.2 Horizon-Aware Interpretation

The same technical signal has different meaning across different horizons.

Example:

Price above MA20:
- Short-term: supportive
- Swing-term: insufficient unless MA60 / swing structure also supports
- Long-term: only minor timing information

Example:

Price below MA60:
- Short-term: weak but may rebound
- Swing-term: important risk
- Long-term: not enough unless MA120 / MA240 and fundamentals also deteriorate

---

## 1.3 Every Non-Neutral Technical View Needs Invalidation

No bullish or bearish technical view may be output without invalidation conditions.

Example:

Short-term bullish view invalidates if:
- close below MA20
- volume expands on breakdown
- breakout point fails
- price falls back into previous range
- short-term institutional buying turns into selling

---

## 1.4 Strong Technical Signal Is Not Equal to High Confidence

A technical signal can be strong but low-confidence if:

- volume is weak
- liquidity is thin
- market regime is bearish
- sector peers are weak
- day-trading ratio is high
- calendar distortion exists
- data is missing
- data is mock / fallback / estimated
- chip flow contradicts price

Example:

A stock may have:
- signal_score = 80
- confidence_score = 45
- risk_score = 75

Meaning:

短線訊號偏強，但由於資料品質、籌碼矛盾或高波動風險，信心不能給太高，追價風險偏高。

---

## 1.5 Technical Rules Are V1 Hypotheses

Every technical rule must be stored as:

is_v1_hypothesis = true
backtest_status = not_tested

Do not claim a technical rule is validated until historical backtesting supports it.

# 2. Technical Analysis by Horizon

## 2.1 Short-Term Technical Analysis

Short-term technical analysis focuses on:

可交易性。

Main question:

現在能不能交易？

Primary inputs:

- MA5
- MA10
- MA20
- volume
- support / resistance
- breakout / false breakout
- RSI
- MACD histogram
- KD
- ATR
- candle behavior
- liquidity
- day-trading noise
- short-term chip confirmation

Short-term technical analysis should answer:

- Is the setup near support or resistance?
- Is chasing risk high?
- Is there enough volume?
- Is the breakout real or false?
- Is the stock too overheated?
- Where is the invalidation point?
- Is liquidity good enough to trade?

Common short-term output language:

- 偏多但不宜追高
- 等待放量突破
- 等待回測 MA20 支撐
- 短線轉弱
- 高風險，避免短線操作
- 中性觀望

---

## 2.2 Swing / Mid-Term Technical Analysis

Swing / mid-term technical analysis focuses on:

趨勢可延續性。

Main question:

這個趨勢能不能繼續？

Primary inputs:

- MA20
- MA60
- swing highs
- swing lows
- volume trend
- breakout and retest
- sector strength
- institutional flow continuity

Swing technical analysis should answer:

- Is MA60 supportive?
- Are swing highs and lows improving?
- Is the trend structure still intact?
- Is volume confirming continuation?
- Is sector strength broad enough?
- Are foreign investors or investment trusts still supporting the move?

Common swing output language:

- 波段偏多，可續觀察
- 波段偏多，但等待回檔
- 波段中性，等待方向
- 波段轉弱，降低追價
- 波段失敗，偏空觀察

---

## 2.3 Long-Term Technical Analysis

Long-term technical analysis is only a timing and risk filter.

Primary inputs:

- MA120
- MA240
- long-term high / low structure
- long-term volume
- distance from long-term averages

Long-term technical analysis must never override:

- revenue
- EPS
- margin
- cash flow
- valuation
- industry trend

Long-term technical analysis should answer:

- Is the long-term price position healthy?
- Is the stock technically overextended?
- Is the current price attractive or overheated from a long-term timing perspective?
- Is long-term structure deteriorating?

Common long-term output language:

- 長期技術結構改善，但估值仍需確認
- 長線標的但短線位置過熱
- 長期技術結構轉弱，需搭配基本面重新評估

## 3.2 Required Derived Candle Fields

Required derived fields:

- daily_return
- gap_percent
- intraday_range
- upper_shadow_ratio
- lower_shadow_ratio
- body_ratio
- close_position_in_range

Suggested formulas:

intraday_range = high - low

body = abs(close - open)

upper_shadow:
- if close >= open: high - close
- else: high - open

lower_shadow:
- if close >= open: open - low
- else: close - low

upper_shadow_ratio = upper_shadow / intraday_range

lower_shadow_ratio = lower_shadow / intraday_range

body_ratio = body / intraday_range

close_position_in_range = (close - low) / (high - low)

If high == low:
- avoid division by zero
- mark candle metrics as unavailable
- reduce confidence if candle metrics are necessary for the conclusion

# 5. Price State Classification

Price state is the foundation of the technical module.

The system must classify the stock into one primary state and optional secondary flags.

---

## 5.1 Strong Uptrend

### Definition

A strong uptrend means price structure, moving averages, and volume all support bullish continuation.

### Possible v1 conditions

- close > MA20
- close > MA60
- MA20_slope > 0
- MA60_slope > 0
- recent swing highs are rising
- recent swing lows are rising
- pullbacks hold above MA20 or MA60
- volume expands on up days or breakouts

### Supporting evidence

- RSI between 50 and 75
- MACD histogram positive or improving
- price not extremely extended from MA20
- sector strength supportive
- chip flow not negative

### Risk flags

- RSI > 75
- large gap-up after long rally
- high-level long upper shadow
- high day-trading ratio
- margin financing expands aggressively
- sector peers weaken

### Interpretation

Technical structure is strong, but if the stock is extended, the system must avoid aggressive chasing language.

### Output language

技術結構偏強，股價維持在 MA20 與 MA60 上方，且波段高低點持續墊高。若後續回檔能守住 MA20 / MA60，且量能未失控，短線與波段結構仍偏多。不過若乖離過大，仍需避免追價。

---

## 5.2 Steady Uptrend

### Definition

A steady uptrend is a healthier, slower uptrend. Price rises along major moving averages without extreme overheating.

### Possible v1 conditions

- close > MA20
- MA20_slope > 0
- close near or moderately above MA20
- MA60 flat-to-up or rising
- pullbacks are volume-shrinking
- no severe upper-shadow reversal

### Interpretation

This is often more sustainable than a vertical rally.

### Output language

技術面呈現緩步偏多，股價沿 MA20 上行，量能未見失控，回檔若維持量縮且不破前低，短線結構仍屬健康。

---

## 5.3 Consolidation

### Definition

Consolidation means price lacks directional conviction.

### Possible v1 conditions

- MA5 / MA10 / MA20 / MA60 are compressed or flat
- price repeatedly crosses moving averages
- volume contracts
- range-bound trading
- MACD / KD signals frequently reverse

### Interpretation

In consolidation, indicators produce more noise. Confidence must be lowered.

Do not treat one-day movement above MA20 as confirmed strengthening.

### Output language

目前股價處於盤整狀態，均線糾結且方向尚未明確。單日站上或跌破均線的訊號可信度較低，需等待放量突破或跌破區間後再確認方向。

---

## 5.4 Early Strengthening

### Definition

Early strengthening means price is attempting to transition from weak / neutral to bullish, but confirmation is incomplete.

### Possible v1 conditions

- close reclaims MA20
- MA20_slope improves from down to flat/up
- RSI recovers above 50
- MACD histogram improves
- volume improves but may not yet be decisive
- MA60 may still be flat/down

### Interpretation

This is a watchlist state, not aggressive confirmation.

### Output language

短線有轉強跡象，股價重新站上 MA20，動能指標改善。不過 MA60 尚未明確轉強，波段結構仍需後續量能與法人買盤確認。

---

## 5.5 Overheated High-Level Trend

### Definition

Overheated trend means bullish structure remains, but risk-reward for new entries is poor.

### Possible v1 conditions

- close far above MA20
- RSI > 70
- KD high-level dulling
- Bollinger upper expansion
- large volume spike
- long upper shadow
- high day-trading ratio
- margin financing increases
- sector or chip confirmation weakens

### Interpretation

Overheated does not mean immediate bearish reversal.

It means:
- trend may still be bullish
- chasing risk is high
- risk-reward for new entries is worse
- wait for pullback or renewed confirmation

### Output language

趨勢仍偏多，但短線位階偏高，乖離與過熱風險上升。若未出現健康回檔或量能續航，追價風險偏高。

---

## 5.6 Early Weakening

### Definition

Early weakening means bullish structure is starting to fail but bearish trend is not fully confirmed.

### Possible v1 conditions

- close below MA20
- MA20_slope flattens or turns down
- RSI falls below 50
- MACD histogram declines
- uptrend support breaks
- volume expands on down days

### Interpretation

Short-term bullish thesis must be reduced or invalidated.

### Output language

短線技術結構開始轉弱，股價跌破 MA20 且動能降溫。若反彈無法站回 MA20，短線偏多假設需下修。

---

## 5.7 Weak Rebound

### Definition

A weak rebound occurs inside a bearish or neutral structure.

### Possible v1 conditions

- price rebounds but remains below MA60
- MA60_slope flat/down
- volume weak on rebound
- RSI fails to reclaim 50 or holds only slightly above
- MACD golden cross occurs below major resistance

### Interpretation

A weak rebound is not a trend reversal.

### Output language

目前較像弱勢反彈，股價尚未站回 MA60，量能與結構仍不足以確認波段反轉。需等待 MA60 與前高壓力有效突破。

---

## 5.8 Downtrend Continuation

### Definition

Downtrend continuation means bearish structure is confirmed.

### Possible v1 conditions

- close < MA20
- close < MA60
- MA20_slope < 0
- MA60_slope < 0
- lower highs and lower lows
- rebounds fail near MA20 / MA60
- down days show stronger volume

### Interpretation

Short/mid-term technical risk is high. One or two rebound days should not be treated as a reversal.

### Output language

技術結構偏空，股價位於 MA20 與 MA60 下方，反彈無法有效站回主要均線。若低點持續下移，短中期風險仍高。

# 6. Trend Logic

## 6.1 Bullish Trend

Bullish trend =
higher highs
+ higher lows
+ price above major moving averages
+ moving average slopes improving
+ volume supports upward movement
+ pullbacks remain controlled

Bullish trend is not:
- one green candle
- one MA crossover
- one MACD golden cross
- one RSI recovery
- one good news headline

---

## 6.2 Bearish Trend

Bearish trend =
lower highs
+ lower lows
+ price below major moving averages
+ moving average slopes weakening
+ rebounds fail
+ selling pressure expands

Bearish trend is not:
- one red candle
- one RSI decline
- one day of institutional selling
- one minor pullback inside a strong uptrend

---

## 6.3 Bullish Trend Confirmation

Bullish confirmation requires multiple signals:

- close above resistance or MA20 / MA60
- higher low forms
- volume expands on breakout
- pullback holds prior resistance or MA20
- RSI > 50
- MACD histogram improves
- sector and market are not hostile
- chip flow does not contradict

---

## 6.4 Bearish Trend Confirmation

Bearish confirmation requires multiple signals:

- close below support or MA20 / MA60
- lower high forms
- breakdown volume expands
- rebound fails
- RSI < 50
- MACD histogram weakens
- chip flow turns negative
- sector weakens

---

## 6.5 Short-Term Bullish Thesis Failure

Short-term bullish thesis fails when:

- close below MA20 with volume expansion
- breakout point fails
- price falls back into previous range
- previous short-term swing low breaks
- institutional buying turns to selling
- sector leadership breaks

---

## 6.6 Swing Bullish Thesis Failure

Swing bullish thesis fails when:

- close below MA60
- MA60 turns flat/down
- previous swing low breaks
- rebound fails to reclaim MA60
- foreign / investment trust flow turns into continuous selling
- sector leadership breaks down

---

# 7. Moving Average System

## 7.1 MA5

Meaning:

Very short-term trading rhythm.

Use:
- detect acceleration
- detect short-term pullback
- measure immediate momentum

Warning:

MA5 is noisy. Do not use alone.

---

## 7.2 MA10

Meaning:

Short-term rhythm smoother than MA5.

Use:
- short-term continuation
- mild pullback tracking
- fast trend quality

Warning:

MA10 is still short-term. It cannot define swing trend alone.

---

## 7.3 MA20

Meaning:

Short-term core line.

Use:
- short-term trend boundary
- pullback support
- short-term invalidation reference

Common logic:

close > MA20 and MA20_slope improving
→ short-term structure improves

close < MA20 with volume expansion
→ short-term bullish thesis weakens

---

## 7.4 MA60

Meaning:

Swing / mid-term core line.

Use:
- swing trend validation
- medium-term support / resistance
- trend continuation filter

Common logic:

close > MA60 and MA60_slope flat-to-up
→ swing structure improves

close < MA60 with expanding volume
→ swing bullish thesis risk rises

---

## 7.5 MA120 / MA240

Meaning:

Long-term technical structure.

Use:
- long-term market cost
- long-term trend condition
- timing filter for long-term holdings

Warning:

Long-term decisions must not rely only on MA120 / MA240. Fundamentals and valuation dominate long-term holdability.

---

## 7.6 Moving Average Bullish Alignment

Bullish alignment:

- MA5 > MA10 > MA20 > MA60
- price above MA20 / MA60
- MA20 and MA60 slopes positive

Interpretation:

Bullish alignment supports trend, but not necessarily entry.

Risk:

If price is far above MA20 / MA60:
- trend remains bullish
- chasing risk rises

---

## 7.7 Moving Average Bearish Alignment

Bearish alignment:

- MA5 < MA10 < MA20 < MA60
- price below MA20 / MA60
- MA20 and MA60 slopes negative

Interpretation:

Bearish alignment indicates market average cost is declining. Rebounds should be treated carefully until structure improves.

---

## 7.8 Moving Average Compression

Definition:

- MA5 / MA10 / MA20 / MA60 are close together
- slopes are flat
- price repeatedly crosses moving averages

Interpretation:

- direction unclear
- indicators noisy
- breakout / breakdown confirmation needed
- confidence lower

---

# 8. Volume-Price Logic

## 8.1 Baseline Volume

Volume must be compared against a baseline.

Suggested baselines:

- volume_MA5
- volume_MA20
- volume_MA60
- average volume during consolidation range
- volume on prior breakout

Suggested v1 thresholds:

- mild expansion: volume > volume_MA20 * 1.2
- clear expansion: volume > volume_MA20 * 1.5
- extreme volume: volume > volume_MA20 * 2.0

These thresholds are v1 hypotheses.

---

## 8.2 Price Up + Volume Up

Healthy when:
- near breakout from consolidation
- not extremely extended
- close near high
- sector supportive
- chip flow supportive

Risky when:
- high-level after large rally
- long upper shadow
- close weak
- institutions sell
- day-trading ratio high
- margin financing increases aggressively

Output language:

價漲量增若發生在突破區且收盤站穩，可提高短線轉強可信度；若發生在高檔且伴隨長上影或籌碼轉弱，則需提高換手與出貨風險。

---

## 8.3 Price Up + Volume Down

Healthy version:
- steady uptrend
- pullback ended
- selling pressure light
- price follows MA20 gradually

Unhealthy version:
- breakout without volume
- near resistance
- new high with weaker volume
- sector does not confirm

Output language:

股價上漲但量能未同步放大，若處於壓力或突破階段，突破可信度不足；若是沿均線緩漲，則可視為賣壓不重，但仍需量能確認。

---

## 8.4 Price Down + Volume Up

Risk version:
- breaks MA20 / MA60
- breaks prior swing low
- close near low
- sector weak
- chip flow negative

Possible absorption version:
- low-level long lower shadow
- support holds
- selling pressure absorbed
- next day does not break low

Output language:

價跌量增代表賣壓放大，若同時跌破支撐或均線，技術風險升高；若出現在低檔且收長下影，需觀察是否為恐慌出量後的承接。

---

## 8.5 Price Down + Volume Down

Healthy pullback:
- uptrend intact
- pullback toward MA20
- volume contracts
- does not break prior low

Weak demand:
- downtrend
- rebound failed
- price drifts lower with weak buying

Output language:

價跌量縮在多頭趨勢中可能是健康回檔，但在空頭或弱勢反彈後，可能只是買盤不足，不能直接判斷為止跌。

---

# 9. Support and Resistance

## 9.1 Support Sources

Support may come from:

- previous swing low
- range lower boundary
- MA20
- MA60
- MA120 / MA240
- prior resistance after breakout
- high-volume zone
- gap lower edge
- round number
- previous day low
- previous week low
- previous month low

---

## 9.2 Resistance Sources

Resistance may come from:

- previous swing high
- range upper boundary
- historical high
- MA20 / MA60 as overhead resistance
- high-volume trapped zone
- gap upper edge
- round number
- previous day high
- previous week high
- previous month high

---

## 9.3 Support / Resistance Strength Factors

Strength depends on:

- number of tests
- volume traded at level
- overlap with moving average
- overlap with prior high / low
- gap presence
- sector confirmation
- chip confirmation

---

## 9.4 Support-Resistance Flip

Resistance broken and retested successfully:
→ resistance becomes support

Support broken and rebound fails:
→ support becomes resistance

---

## 9.5 Effective Support

Support is effective only if:

- price holds support
- or breakdown quickly recovers
- volume does not lose control
- rebound has buying support
- chip flow does not strongly contradict

---

## 9.6 Failed Support

Support likely fails when:

- close below support
- volume expands
- rebound fails
- price forms lower low
- institutional flow weakens

---

# 10. Breakout / Breakdown / False Signals

## 10.1 Healthy Breakout

Conditions:

- close above resistance
- volume > volume_MA20
- strong candle body
- not a long upper shadow
- no quick fall back into range
- prior resistance holds on pullback
- no major institutional selling
- market / sector not hostile
- liquidity quality acceptable
- day-trading noise not excessive

Output language:

若股價放量站上前高並能回測不破，且法人籌碼未轉弱，短線突破可信度提高。

---

## 10.2 Breakout Pending Confirmation

Conditions:

- price closes slightly above resistance
- volume only mildly expands
- sector not clearly supportive
- chip flow neutral

Output language:

股價雖突破壓力，但量能與籌碼確認不足，仍需後續 1～3 日確認是否站穩。

---

## 10.3 False Breakout Risk

Conditions:

- intraday breakout but close back below resistance
- breakout volume weak
- long upper shadow
- next day falls back into range
- institutions sell
- margin financing rises
- day-trading ratio high
- sector peers do not confirm

Output language:

股價雖一度突破前高，但收盤未能站穩且量能不足，假突破風險升高，短線不宜追價。

---

## 10.4 Effective Breakdown

Conditions:

- close below support / MA20 / MA60
- volume expands
- rebound fails
- lower low forms
- chip flow turns negative
- market or sector weakens

Output language:

股價跌破關鍵支撐且量能放大，若後續反彈無法站回，短線或波段結構需下修。

---

## 10.5 False Breakdown Possible

Conditions:

- intraday breakdown but close recovers
- long lower shadow
- volume spike at support
- next day does not break low
- chip flow does not worsen

Output language:

股價盤中跌破支撐但收盤收回，暫不宜判斷為有效跌破，需觀察後續是否站穩支撐。

---

# 11. Momentum Indicators

## 11.1 RSI

Interpretation:

RSI > 70:
- overheating risk
- not automatic bearish

RSI 50–70:
- healthy bullish momentum zone

RSI 40–50:
- neutral weak

RSI < 40:
- bearish momentum stronger

RSI < 30:
- oversold
- not automatic bullish

Rules:

RSI > 70 + strong uptrend:
- trend strong
- chasing risk rises

RSI > 70 + resistance + long upper shadow:
- short-term reversal risk rises

RSI recovers above 50 + reclaim MA20:
- short-term strengthening support

RSI falls below 50 + break MA20:
- short-term weakening support

Important:

RSI must be interpreted with price state.

Strong uptrend + high RSI does not automatically mean bearish.

Downtrend + low RSI does not automatically mean bullish.

---

## 11.2 MACD

Use:

MACD confirms trend momentum.

Important rules:

MACD golden cross below MA60:
- often weak rebound
- not swing reversal

MACD golden cross + reclaim MA20 + volume improves:
- short-term strengthening signal

MACD death cross in strong uptrend:
- momentum cooling
- not automatic trend failure

MACD histogram contraction:
- momentum weakening
- not necessarily reversal

MACD histogram turning negative + support break:
- short-term weakness confirmation

---

## 11.3 KD

Use:

KD is short-term rhythm and very noisy in strong trends.

Rules:

KD high-level dulling in strong uptrend:
- not automatic bearish

KD death cross near resistance:
- short-term pullback risk

KD golden cross near support:
- possible short-term bounce

KD golden cross below MA60:
- weak rebound unless structure improves

---

## 11.4 Bollinger Bands

Use:

Bollinger bands help identify volatility compression and expansion.

Rules:

Band contraction:
- volatility compression
- wait for direction

Band expansion upward + confirmed breakout:
- possible trend start

Upper band touch:
- not automatic sell

Lower band touch:
- not automatic buy

Bollinger contraction + declining volume:
- direction unclear
- wait for breakout / breakdown

Bollinger expansion + close above upper band + volume expansion:
- possible bullish volatility expansion

Bollinger expansion + long upper shadow:
- possible exhaustion / distribution risk

---

## 11.5 ATR

Use:

ATR measures volatility, not direction.

Rules:

ATR expansion + breakout:
- possible trend start

ATR expansion + breakdown:
- risk rises

ATR expansion + high-level upper shadow:
- high volatility distribution risk

ATR contraction:
- waiting state / consolidation

ATR should adjust risk, not direction.

---

# 12. Overheating, Deviation, and Divergence

## 12.1 Overheating

Overheating means new-entry risk is high.

It does not mean immediate bearish reversal.

Conditions:

- price far above MA20
- RSI > 70
- KD high-level dulling
- Bollinger upper expansion
- volume spike
- gap-up after rally
- high-level long upper shadow
- margin financing increase
- day-trading ratio high
- chip flow weakens

Interpretation:

- trend may not be broken
- chasing risk rises
- risk-reward deteriorates
- wait for pullback or renewed confirmation

Output language:

短線位階偏高，雖然趨勢尚未破壞，但追價風險升高，較適合等待回檔或量能再次確認。

---

## 12.2 Deviation from MA20 / MA60

Suggested fields:

deviation_from_MA20_pct = (close / MA20 - 1) * 100

deviation_from_MA60_pct = (close / MA60 - 1) * 100

Interpretation:

High deviation:
- profit-taking risk rises
- risk-reward deteriorates
- new entries less attractive

Low deviation in uptrend:
- healthier pullback
- potential support test

Thresholds must be calibrated by stock volatility and sector.

---

## 12.3 Price-Momentum Divergence

Bearish divergence:

- price makes new high
- RSI does not make new high
- MACD histogram declines
- volume does not expand

Interpretation:

Momentum is weakening, not immediate bearish confirmation.

Output language:

股價創高但動能指標未同步創高，代表上攻動能降溫，需降低追價信心並觀察是否出現量價失衡。

---

## 12.4 Price-Volume Divergence

Conditions:

- price breaks recent high
- volume lower than volume_MA20
- or volume lower than prior breakout volume

Interpretation:

Breakout credibility is weak.

Output language:

股價突破但量能未同步確認，突破可信度不足，需等待後續量能或回測支撐確認。

---

## 12.5 Price-Chip Divergence

### Technical Strong, Chip Weak

Conditions:

- price rises
- foreign / investment trust sells
- margin rises
- day-trading ratio high

Output language:

技術價格偏強，但籌碼品質不足，短線可觀察但不宜高信心追價。

### Chip Strong, Price Weak

Conditions:

- foreign / investment trust buys
- price flat or weak
- volume not supportive

Output language:

法人買盤雖然偏正向，但股價尚未有效反應，可能仍有上方賣壓或籌碼吸收，需等待價格表態。

# 13. Volatility and Risk

## 13.1 Volatility States

Suggested states:

- normal_volatility
- compressed_volatility
- expanding_volatility
- high_risk_volatility

---

## 13.2 Expanding Volatility

ATR expansion + breakout + volume + chip support:
→ constructive

ATR expansion + breakdown + weak chip:
→ dangerous

ATR expansion + high-level upper shadow:
→ distribution / exhaustion risk

---

## 13.3 Volatility Risk Adjustment

Technical risk should increase when:

- ATR_ratio rises sharply
- price breaks support with expanding ATR
- price forms high-level upper shadow with high ATR
- wide-range candle closes weak
- gap-up fails
- gap-down cannot recover

# 14. Candlestick and Gap Logic

## 14.1 Long Bullish Candle

Constructive when:

- after consolidation
- volume expands
- close near high
- breaks resistance
- chip flow supportive

Risky when:

- after extended rally
- extreme volume
- gap-up
- next day fails
- long upper shadow appears

---

## 14.2 Long Bearish Candle

Risky when:

- at high level
- volume expands
- breaks MA20
- breaks prior low
- chip flow weakens

Not necessarily bearish when:

- after long decline
- near support
- followed by long lower shadow
- next day stabilizes

---

## 14.3 Long Upper Shadow

Meaning:

Selling pressure appeared.

High-risk when:

- near resistance
- high-level
- volume spike
- breakout failed
- institutions sell
- day-trading ratio high

---

## 14.4 Long Lower Shadow

Meaning:

Buying support appeared.

Constructive only if:

- near support
- next day does not break low
- price reclaims short MA
- selling volume cools
- chip flow does not worsen

---

## 14.5 Gap-Up

Breakaway gap:

- after consolidation
- above resistance
- volume confirms
- does not fill quickly

Exhaustion gap:

- after long rally
- opens high
- huge volume
- long upper shadow / black candle
- fails to continue

---

## 14.6 Gap-Down

Risky if:

- breaks support
- volume expands
- cannot recover gap
- chip flow weakens

Possible exhaustion if:

- after long decline
- near major support
- large lower shadow appears
- volume spikes
- next day stabilizes

---

# 15. Technical Invalidation Rules

## 15.1 Short-Term Technical Invalidation

Short-term technical bullish view invalidates when:

- close below MA20 with volume expansion
- breakout fails and price returns to range
- high-level long upper shadow with volume spike
- RSI loses 50 after failed breakout
- MACD histogram turns negative after support break
- day-trading noise leads to next-day selling
- short-term institutional buying turns into selling

---

## 15.2 Swing Technical Invalidation

Swing technical bullish view invalidates when:

- close below MA60
- MA60_slope turns negative
- previous swing low breaks
- rebound fails to reclaim MA60
- volume expands on down days
- sector leadership breaks
- institutional buying turns into continuous selling

---

## 15.3 Long-Term Technical Invalidation

Long-term technical support weakens when:

- close below MA120 / MA240
- MA120 / MA240 slopes deteriorate
- long-term higher-low structure breaks
- long-term volume distribution appears

Long-term technical invalidation alone should not override fundamentals, but it should increase timing risk and monitoring priority.

# 17. Technical Confidence Rules

## 17.1 Confidence Increases When

Technical confidence increases when:

- price structure is clear
- moving averages are aligned
- volume confirms
- breakout / breakdown is confirmed
- momentum supports
- market regime is supportive
- sector confirms
- liquidity is sufficient
- traceability is complete

---

## 17.2 Confidence Decreases When

Technical confidence decreases when:

- moving averages are compressed
- volume is weak
- price is near midpoint of range
- momentum indicators conflict
- breakout lacks volume
- high-level upper shadow appears
- sector peers weaken
- liquidity is thin
- day-trading ratio is high
- calendar distortion exists
- data is missing or fallback
- reason cannot be traced

---

## 17.3 Confidence Caps

Mock / fallback data:
- max confidence = medium_low

Estimated data:
- max confidence = medium

Single indicator signal:
- no high confidence

No invalidation signal:
- no high confidence

Low liquidity:
- no aggressive short-term strategy

High day-trading noise:
- reduce short-term confidence

---

# 18. Technical Risk Rules

## 18.1 Risk Increases When

Technical risk increases when:

- price far above MA20
- RSI > 70
- high-level volume spike
- long upper shadow
- breakout lacks confirmation
- close below MA20 / MA60 with volume
- ATR expands against direction
- support breaks
- sector weakens
- chip flow contradicts price
- low liquidity
- calendar distortion
- day-trading noise

---

## 18.2 Risk Decreases When

Technical risk decreases when:

- pullback holds support
- volume contracts on pullback
- sector confirms
- liquidity sufficient
- chip flow supports
- price not overextended

# 20. Backtestable Technical Hypotheses

## 20.1 Breakout Volume Confirmation

rule_id:

tech_breakout_volume_confirm

hypothesis:

A close above 20-day resistance with volume above volume_MA20 improves forward 20-day return.

signal_condition:

- close > recent_high_20d
- volume / volume_MA20 >= 1.0

invalidation:

- close falls back below breakout level

---

## 20.2 Breakout Failure

rule_id:

tech_breakout_failure

hypothesis:

Intraday breakout that closes back inside the range increases 5-day downside risk.

signal_condition:

- high > resistance
- close < resistance

---

## 20.3 MA20 Support Hold

rule_id:

tech_ma20_support_hold

hypothesis:

Pullback that holds MA20 with shrinking volume supports continuation.

signal_condition:

- low near MA20
- close >= MA20
- pullback volume declines

invalidation:

- close < MA20 with expanding volume

---

## 20.4 MA60 Swing Breakdown

rule_id:

tech_ma60_break_swing

hypothesis:

Close below MA60 on expanding volume invalidates swing-term bullish thesis.

signal_condition:

- close < MA60
- volume / volume_MA20 >= 1.5

---

## 20.5 RSI Overheating Reversal Risk

rule_id:

tech_rsi_overheating

hypothesis:

RSI > 70 plus high-level long upper shadow increases short-term reversal risk.

signal_condition:

- RSI > 70
- upper_shadow_ratio >= 0.5
- price level near recent high

---

## 20.6 False Breakout After Low Volume

rule_id:

tech_false_breakout_low_volume

hypothesis:

Breakout without sufficient volume has higher false-breakout probability.

signal_condition:

- close > resistance
- volume / volume_MA20 < 1.0

---

## 20.7 Healthy Pullback

rule_id:

tech_healthy_pullback

hypothesis:

In an uptrend, price pullback to MA20 with volume contraction has positive continuation probability.

signal_condition:

- close > MA60
- MA20_slope > 0
- low near MA20
- volume < volume_MA20

---

## 20.8 High-Level Volume Exhaustion

rule_id:

tech_high_level_volume_exhaustion

hypothesis:

High-level extreme volume with weak close increases short-term reversal risk.

signal_condition:

- price near 60-day high
- volume / volume_MA20 >= 2.0
- close_position_in_range < 0.5

---

## 20.9 Early Strengthening Confirmation

rule_id:

tech_early_strengthening_confirm

hypothesis:

Reclaiming MA20 with RSI above 50 and improving MACD histogram increases short-term continuation probability.

signal_condition:

- close > MA20
- RSI > 50
- MACD_histogram improving over last 3 bars

---

## 20.10 Weak Rebound Failure

rule_id:

tech_weak_rebound_failure

hypothesis:

Rebounds below a declining MA60 with weak volume have poor swing continuation.

signal_condition:

- close < MA60

- rebound volume < volume_MA20
